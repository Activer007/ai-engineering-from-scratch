Sampling uses x = x - dt * v while t decreases. Preserve all signs, time directions and code exactly. Do not translate the velocity target as a noise-to-data vector merely because sampling travels backward.
2. The sentence saying AdaLNZero itself starts as an identity mapping is overbroad relative to the supplied code. AdaLNZero returns normalized h and a zero gate; the residual DiTBlock becomes identity through zero gates. Record this distinction as a source issue; do not silently change the English referent in translation or alter protected code.
3. The source treats straight interpolation, a learned marginal ODE and a straight sampling trajectory as near-equivalent, and gives broad 20-versus-1000 quality claims without an experiment here. Preserve the source's level of assertion, not a new theorem, guarantee or measured result. The model's regression target is a velocity, not DDPM epsilon prediction.
